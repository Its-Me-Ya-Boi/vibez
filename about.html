The beginning

Why we
built this.

Vibez was created out of a simple frustration: existing platforms were too noisy, too algorithmic, too focused on engagement metrics over genuine human moments.

We wanted something different — a place where your feed feels intentional, where your profile reflects you, and where the people you care about are always a filter away.

So we built it ourselves. From a single developer writing the first lines of PHP to the platform you're using today, every feature has been shaped by the question: "does this make connections feel more real?"

What we stand for

Our values.

◈

Authenticity first

We don't boost posts or push content to maximize time-on-site. What you see is what people actually shared.

✦

Your space

Your profile is yours to customize. Make it feel like you, not a template.

❋

Meaningful circles

The friends vs. following distinction matters to us. Mutual connections get their own place in your feed.

◉

Keep it simple

No dark patterns, no manipulative notifications, no infinite scroll traps. Just posts, people, and conversations.

How we got here

The journey.

October 2025
Launch

Vibez went live for the first time. Basic posting, user accounts, and an admin panel were the foundation.

November 2025
Photos & videos

Media posting landed. Users could now attach images and videos to their posts.

Late 2025
Following & friends

The social graph arrived. Follow people, build mutual friendships, and filter your feed by relationship.

Early 2026
Profile customization

Full color and background customization came to profile pages. Every user now has a truly unique space.

Now
Still building

Close friends roles, a refined UI, and more are in active development. The best is still ahead.
