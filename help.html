Getting started

How do I create a Vibez account?
Head to the registration page and fill in your desired username, email address, and a password. Once submitted you'll be taken straight to your feed — no email confirmation required.
What does Vibez cost?
Nothing — Vibez is completely free to join and use. There are no paid tiers or hidden charges. Ads may be added in the future to support the platform, but a one-time payment option will be available.
I just signed up. Where do I start?
We recommend three things right away: upload a profile picture in Settings, make your first post using the post menu in the header, and follow a few people from the sidebar on your feed.
What browsers does Vibez support?
Any modern browser works — Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge are all fully supported. Vibez is also mobile-friendly and adapts to smaller screens automatically.

Account & profile

How do I change my username?
Go to Settings and fill in the "Change username" field, then hit submit. Your new name will appear on all your existing posts immediately.
How do I update my profile picture?
In Settings, click "Pick profile image," choose a JPG, PNG, or GIF file from your device, and submit. Supported formats: jpg, jpeg, png, gif.
Can I customize the colors on my profile page?
Yes! Settings lets you pick colors for your background, buttons, posts, comments, text, and username separately. You can also upload a background image. Changes apply only to your profile page and are visible to anyone who visits it.
How do I change my password or email?
Both can be updated from the Settings page — look for the "Change password" and "Change email" forms. Passwords are stored securely and are never visible to administrators.
How do I delete my account?
Scroll to the bottom of the Settings page and click the Delete button. This action is permanent — all your posts, comments, and profile data will be removed.

Posts & media

What kinds of posts can I make?
Three types: a plain text post, a post with an attached image, or a post with an attached video. Use the post dropdown in the navigation header to choose.
What file types and sizes are allowed?
Images: jpg, jpeg, png, gif. Videos: mp4, mov. The maximum file size is 20 MB. Files exceeding this limit will not upload.
Can I delete a post after publishing?
Yes. Click the delete link at the bottom-right of any of your own posts. You'll be asked to confirm before the post is permanently removed.
How do comments work?
Click "View comments" under any post to open the comments page. Type your comment in the box and press submit. All logged-in users can comment on any post. You can delete any comment from the comment view.

Following & friends

What's the difference between following and friends?
Following is one-directional — you follow someone and see their posts. Friends are mutual: when two users each follow the other, they become friends. The friends filter on the main feed shows only posts from mutual connections.
How do I follow someone?
Visit their profile page (click their name anywhere on the site) and tap the follow button. The button will change to "following" to confirm. Tap it again at any time to unfollow.
What are the feed filters?
The left sidebar on the main feed has four filters: close friends, friends (mutual follows), following (everyone you follow), and everyone (the full public feed).

Privacy & safety

Who can see my posts?
All posts are visible to everyone on Vibez who is logged in. There are currently no private or restricted-visibility posts — all content is public within the platform.
Can I report or block a user?
If you encounter abusive behavior, please contact a site administrator directly. Admins have the ability to ban accounts. A self-service report feature is on our roadmap.
How do I switch between light and dark mode?
Click the light/dark mode button in Settings. Your preference is saved in your browser and will persist across sessions.

Technical issues

My file won't upload
Check that: (1) the file is under 20 MB, (2) the format is supported (jpg, png, gif, mp4, or mov), and (3) a file with the same name doesn't already exist on the server. Try renaming the file if the problem persists.
The page looks broken or unstyled.
Try a hard refresh: Ctrl + Shift + R (Windows/Linux) or Cmd + Shift + R (Mac). If the problem persists, clear your browser cache and try again. Still broken? Reach out to the team.
I was logged out unexpectedly.
Vibez sessions expire when you close your browser tab. Simply sign in again. If you're being logged out mid-session, check that your browser accepts cookies from this site.
Light/dark mode isn't saving between pages.
Vibez uses localStorage to remember your preference. Make sure your browser isn't set to clear storage on close, and that you haven't blocked local storage for this site.
